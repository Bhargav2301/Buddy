## Result and limits

Both remaining frozen refinement utility cases now produce a useful, modest structural improvement: the entire request and its explicit trailing constraint become separate reviewed sections. Names, emoji, currencies, numbers, units, negation and the original semicolon remain exact. No general rewrite quality, understanding of arbitrary English scope, or natural-language safety guarantee is claimed.

The browser foundation is implemented and tested with synthetic content, real Chromium DOM primitives and an actual JavaScript-to-C# protocol connection. **The production ChatGPT adapter remains readiness-only and incomplete for live history, draft insertion and original attachments.** Public documentation and the available local evidence do not establish a stable, verified account/workspace/history-completion/upload contract. The host therefore admits no production identity profile. User-confirmed display labels, a route, an attach button or a missing spinner do not bypass that boundary. Other sites used in Comet require their own capability profiles; Comet is a browser, not a universal chat destination.
