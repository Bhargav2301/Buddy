## Selected 0.4.5 work and acceptance boundary

The selected batch combines Guide/window and inline-refinement repairs, a bounded exact-app opening adapter, and usable advanced refinement in **Buddy-owned drafts**. Shared deterministic preflight, supporting inputs and rationale, explicit counts/limits, reviewed text resources, warnings and request-revision binding are included. The external quick overlay gains no-op rejection and a bounded footer while preserving focus and exact Undo; advanced external-field controls remain missing.

Implementation and independent acceptance must establish that:

1. Automatic/manual choices use the same production prerequisite rules; examples/tools/stages are not fabricated to make a technique appear ready.
2. Every limit names its count unit and explicit source. Required intent and confirmed constraints survive, optional content is omitted visibly, and unavoidable conflicts keep the original rather than clipping it.
3. Local resources are explicitly selected, bounded, reviewable and removable; malformed/unsupported content is refused. No links are followed, files watched, credentials read, accounts connected or permanent knowledge silently added.
4. Every draft/mode/technique/input/budget/resource change invalidates old acceptance, including during inference. Stop and late-result rejection cover the complete request. Apply/Undo retain the exact intended draft.
5. Preflight readiness means supported inputs and a fitting destination budget, not guaranteed model success. Local inference-context limits, final fidelity and output-budget validation still apply.

The scoped implementation now has integrated service and owned-native evidence in [0.4.5](Windows-0.4.5.md). Keep those passes separate from limited actual-model usefulness, incomplete current Comet observation and still-open physical/actual-browser acceptance.

Subsequent local priorities are Guide imported-knowledge consistency; a requested-long-answer override retaining the ordinary three-sentence default and complete safety review; local session identity/state infrastructure with injected fixtures before any hook setup; and actual-model early-speech acceptance. Advanced original-application refinement remains an explicit workflow gap alongside those priorities. No hook installation, grant or provider connection follows from local session infrastructure.
